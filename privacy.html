Privacy Policy — Mandi Commodity Info

Last updated: 21 September 2026

Mandi Commodity Info provides agricultural commodity and mandi price information through WhatsApp.

Information We Collect

We may collect your WhatsApp phone number, name if provided, messages sent to the service, and your selected market and commodity subscriptions.

How We Use Information

We use this information to provide requested mandi prices, manage your subscriptions, respond to WhatsApp messages, and provide price notifications when you have subscribed to them.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers required to operate the WhatsApp messaging and price information service.

Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, maintain subscriptions, and comply with applicable requirements.

Your Choices

You may stop using the service at any time. You may also request removal of your stored subscription information.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning your information, please contact the operator of Mandi Commodity Info through the WhatsApp service.
